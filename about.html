Who we are
and how this works

A family owned company with three restaurants, 118 employees and a business model we are happy to write down in public.

Founded 1954 Three rooms 118 employees Family owned, never franchised Prices published everywhere

The company

Starlite Chrome Diner LLC is a privately held New Mexico limited liability company, registered in Bernalillo County and owned by the Vasquez family since it was founded in 1954. We have never franchised, never taken outside investment, and never operated a location we did not own or lease directly.

There are three restaurants. Albuquerque is the original and also functions as our commissary: every pie and every batch of Chrome Sauce in the company is made there. Chicago opened in 2011 and Santa Monica in 2019.

We employ 118 people. Roughly 60 percent are full time, and average tenure across the company is a little over four years, which for this industry is unusually long and is the number we are proudest of.

How the business makes money

Most restaurants keep this vague. We think a guest who understands where the margin comes from makes better choices, so here it is in order of how much of our revenue each line represents.

Revenue mix across all three rooms, twelve months to June 2026
Revenue lineShareHow it is priced
Dine in food and drink 61% Menu price plus local sales tax. No service charge under eight guests. 20% gratuity on parties of eight or more, disclosed in advance.
Takeout and first party delivery 14% Identical menu prices. Pickup free. Delivery flat $4.95 inside four miles. No service or small order fees.
Third party delivery apps 9% The platform sets the guest price, typically around 20% above ours, and charges us 15 to 30% commission. We do not control either number.
Catering and private events 10% Per guest packages at $18, $28 or $42, plus room fees where applicable. Every quote itemised. 20% gratuity to the team working it.
Chef's table 3% $85 per seat with gratuity included, optional $40 beverage flight. Eight seats, three nights a week.
Retail and gift cards 3% Sauce, coffee and merchandise at fixed shelf prices. Gift cards are a liability, not revenue, until they are spent.

Our pricing commitments

  • Every price is published before you order, on this site and on the printed menu.
  • We do not add service fees, kitchen fees, wellness fees, credit card surcharges or inflation surcharges. If a cost rises, the menu price rises and you can see it.
  • The only automatic gratuity is 20% on parties of eight or more, and it is stated when you book, printed on the menu, and shown as its own line on the check.
  • Gift cards carry no fees of any kind and never expire.
  • We have no affiliate links anywhere on this site and earn nothing from any outbound link.

What we hold ourselves to

Food safety

All three kitchens are inspected by their local health department on the standard schedule. Every current inspection score is posted at the entrance to each room and we will hand you the full report if you ask at the register. Every manager holds a ServSafe Manager certification and every hourly employee holds a food handler card, both paid for by us.

Allergens

Every server can name the allergens in every dish, and it is part of the two week training and the six month refresher. We cannot promise a dish is free of any allergen because our kitchens are shared spaces. That limitation is set out honestly on the allergen and nutrition page rather than buried.

Sourcing

We name our suppliers on the our story page and we pay them list price. We do not accept placement fees to feature a product, and no supplier has ever paid to be on our menu. If we ever changed that, this paragraph would change with it.

Advertising and reviews

Every quote on this site is from a real named guest who gave permission. We do not buy reviews, incentivise them, or filter them. We run advertising on this website, which is labelled as advertising and kept clearly separate from our own content.

Accessibility

All three rooms have step free entry and accessible restrooms. This website is built to WCAG 2.1 Level AA and we test it with a keyboard and a screen reader before each change. Details and known gaps are on the accessibility statement.

Your data

We collect the minimum needed to take a booking, fill an order or run Chrome Club. We do not sell or share personal information for cross context behavioural advertising. Full detail, including your CCPA and CPRA rights, is in the Privacy Policy.
