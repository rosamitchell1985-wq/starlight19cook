The short version

We cannot guarantee that any dish we serve is free from any allergen. Our three kitchens are small shared spaces. The same griddle, fryers, prep surfaces, utensils and staff handle wheat, dairy, egg, soy, peanuts, tree nuts, fish, shellfish and sesame every single day.

If your allergy is severe or anaphylactic, please talk to us before you order so we can tell you honestly whether we can serve you safely. Sometimes the honest answer is no, and we would rather say that than take a risk with you.

What our menu tags mean

Tags on the menu and the drinks list describe the recipe as written. They are not a guarantee about cross contact.

Dietary tag definitions
TagMeansDoes not mean
GF Gluten free No gluten containing ingredient is used in the recipe as it is written and served. Not certified gluten free. Prepared in a kitchen where wheat flour is used daily. Not suitable for coeliac disease without a conversation with us first.
V Vegetarian No meat, poultry or fish in the recipe. May contain dairy or egg. Does not mean cooked on separate equipment unless the dish says so. Our fries are cooked in dedicated vegetable oil; the flat top is shared.
VG Vegan No animal product at all in the recipe: no meat, fish, dairy, egg or honey. Does not mean a completely separate cooking environment, although we do use a dedicated griddle section and clean tools for vegan items.
N Contains nuts The recipe contains peanuts or tree nuts as written. The absence of this tag does not mean a dish is nut free. Nuts are handled throughout the pastry section.

Allergens present in our kitchens

All nine major food allergens recognised under United States federal law are handled daily in all three of our kitchens:

  • Milk. Butter, cheese, cream, buttermilk, custard base, malt powder blends.
  • Eggs. Breakfast service, custard base, mayonnaise, Chrome Sauce, breading.
  • Fish. Cod for the Friday special, albacore for the tuna melt, anchovy in some dressings.
  • Shellfish. Shrimp on the chef's table, clam in the green chile chowder shot.
  • Tree nuts. Pecans, hazelnuts, cashews in the pastry and vegan sections.
  • Peanuts. Peanut butter shakes, salted peanuts on sundaes and the banana split.
  • Wheat. Buns, rye, biscuits, pancake and doughnut batter, breading, crackers.
  • Soy. Frying oil blends, some vegan cheeses, several sauces, soy milk.
  • Sesame. Some buns, tahini in one dressing, sesame oil in the grain bowl.

We also handle mustard, celery, sulphites and lupin, which are declarable allergens in some other countries and which we will happily confirm for any dish.

Cross contact, specifically

Rather than hiding behind a general phrase, here is exactly where cross contact happens in our kitchens:

  • The flat top griddle cooks beef, vegan patties, eggs, pancakes, onions and buttered bread on the same surface, separated by section but not by barrier. It is scraped between orders, not deep cleaned.
  • Fryers. We run two. One is dedicated to fries and vegetable items and contains no animal product. The second fries chicken, fish, pickles and doughnuts and is shared between all of them.
  • The pastry section handles wheat flour, nuts and dairy on the same bench. Flour is airborne there.
  • The fountain uses shared spindles and rinse wells for dairy shakes, peanut butter shakes and dairy free sorbet floats.
  • Toasters and salamanders are shared between gluten containing and gluten free breads.

What we can do when you tell us about an allergy: change gloves, use a clean pan or a clean section of the griddle, pull ingredients from unopened containers, use dedicated utensils, and have the manager on duty personally check the plate before it leaves the pass. For many allergies that is genuinely sufficient. For an airborne or anaphylactic allergy in a room this small, it may not be, and we will tell you so.

How to order with an allergy

  1. Tell us when you book. The booking form has a notes field. It reaches the kitchen before you arrive, which is worth a great deal on a busy service.
  2. Tell your server, out loud, before ordering. Not at the end. Every server is trained to stop and take the question to the kitchen.
  3. Be specific. "Dairy allergy, anaphylactic" and "I am avoiding dairy" lead to genuinely different handling, and we would rather know which one you mean.
  4. Ask for the manager if you want the plate personally checked. It is not an imposition and they will not sigh.
  5. For takeout and delivery, put it in the notes field and call the kitchen as well. Bags are packed quickly and a written note is easy to miss on a Friday.

Nutrition information

Where we publish calorie or nutrition figures, they are estimates calculated from standard recipes using USDA reference data. They are not laboratory tested.

Real variation comes from portioning by hand, seasonal differences in produce, how much sauce a particular cook uses, and substitutions you request. A plate can reasonably vary by 15 to 20 percent from the published figure, and we would rather tell you that than present a number to two decimal places that implies a precision we do not have.

Full nutrition data for any dish is available on request. Email hello@starlitechrome.com and we will send the ingredient list and our best estimate, usually the same day.

Nothing on this site is dietary or medical advice. If you are managing a medical condition through diet, please work from your clinician's guidance and use our ingredient lists as input to that, not as a substitute for it.

Recipes and suppliers change

Ingredients and suppliers change, sometimes at short notice when something is unavailable. A dish that was gluten free last month may not be this month if we had to switch a component. We update this site and our printed menus when that happens, but there is inevitably a gap.

Always confirm with your server on the day. The person standing in front of you has better information than this web page does.

Alcohol, caffeine and other cautions

  • Alcoholic drinks are marked on the drinks list and served only to guests 21 and over with photo identification.
  • Our cold brew is strong. We cap it at two per guest for that reason.
  • Beer and wine contain sulphites. Our house beer contains barley and wheat.
  • Some desserts contain alcohol as a flavouring, including the brandied cherry on the zero proof cherry cola. Ask if this matters to you.
  • Consuming raw or undercooked meat, poultry, seafood, shellfish or eggs may increase your risk of foodborne illness, especially if you have certain medical conditions. Our burgers are cooked through by default; the only item we will serve less than well done is the chef's table steak course, on request.

Disclaimer

The allergen and nutrition information on this website is provided for general guidance. Starlite Chrome Diner LLC makes no warranty, express or implied, that any dish is free from any specific allergen, and does not accept liability for any adverse reaction arising from consumption of our food where you have not informed us of an allergy before ordering.

This is not an attempt to shift responsibility onto you. It is a statement of a physical limit: in a kitchen of this size, with this equipment, a guarantee of absence is not something we can honestly give. Everything within that limit, we will do.
