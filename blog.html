Why we write any of this

Restaurants tend to be secretive about method, usually for no good reason. We take the opposite view. A person who understands why we grind chuck with short rib, or why a malt needs a spindle instead of a blender, is a better guest and generally a happier one. It also keeps us honest: if a technique cannot survive being written down and read by a professional, it probably was not a technique, it was a habit.

Pieces are published when they are finished, not on a schedule, which works out to roughly one a month. Nothing here is sponsored, and we have no affiliate links anywhere on this site. If we name a supplier it is because we buy from them at full price.
