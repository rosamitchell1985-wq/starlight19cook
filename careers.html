Work the line

Every pay range on this page is the real range. We post it because guessing wastes your time and ours, and because we would rather compete on the job than on the ambiguity.

Posted pay ranges Health cover from 25 hours Tips pooled and posted Paid meal every shift No unpaid trail shifts

What the job is actually like

It is a diner. It is hot, it is loud, weekends are relentless, and the griddle station in Albuquerque runs at 450 degrees eighteen inches from your face. Anyone who tells you otherwise is recruiting, not describing.

What we can offer honestly is that the hours are stable, the schedule is posted 14 days ahead and does not change without your agreement, and nobody is sent home early because the room went quiet. If you are rostered, you are paid for the shift.

We also do not run unpaid trail shifts. If we ask you to come in and cook, you are on the clock from the moment you walk in, whether or not you get the job.

How we hire

Send the form

Below, or hand it to anyone at the counter on paper. Every application gets a reply within five business days, including the ones that are a no. Being left in silence is the worst part of job hunting and it costs us nothing to avoid it.

Come and talk

Twenty minutes with the manager of the room, usually mid afternoon between services. It is a conversation, not a test. Bring questions, particularly about money and hours.

Work a paid shift

Four hours, on the clock, on the station you applied for. You find out whether you like the job and we find out whether the job suits you. Either of us can say no afterwards with no hard feelings.

Everyone gets an answer Within five business days

We are an equal opportunity employer. We hire without regard to race, colour, religion, sex, sexual orientation, gender identity, national origin, age, disability, veteran status or any other protected characteristic.

Apply
