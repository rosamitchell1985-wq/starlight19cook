The fountain
and the bar

Six spindles on the counter, a short beer list from breweries within a hundred miles of each room, and cocktails built on the same syrups we use for the sodas.

Shakes spun to order Coffee is bottomless Seltzer pulled from the tap Zero proof list is not an afterthought Full bar in Chicago and Santa Monica

Where you can drink what

The fountain, coffee and zero proof lists are identical in all three rooms. Beer, wine and cocktails are served in Chicago and Santa Monica. Albuquerque holds a beer and wine licence only, so no spirits are poured there, including in the spiked shakes.

Serving alcohol responsibly

You must be 21 or older to be served alcohol and we card everyone who looks under 40. We reserve the right to stop service at any point. If you need a ride home, ask any member of staff and we will call one for you at no cost to you.
