Booking a table

No. The counter is always walk-in and we have never taken a booking for a stool in seventy years. Booths can be held online for a $10 deposit that comes straight off your check.

Practically: a Tuesday at 2pm, just walk in. A Friday at 7:30pm, book. Saturday breakfast in Santa Monica is the busiest service in the company and a booking is worth having.

Fifteen minutes. After that it goes back to the floor and we will seat you at the next opening, usually within twenty minutes. Call the room if you are running late and we will hold it longer where the book allows.

Nine or more goes through private events rather than the normal booking form. That is not an upsell, it is just how we hold the space. You can still order off the standard menu at standard prices.

Eight seats, Thursday to Saturday at 9:30pm, Albuquerque only. $85 per person with gratuity included, prepaid at booking and refundable up to 72 hours ahead. Request a stool through the reservations page or by calling the room. It usually books out two to three weeks ahead.

Money, fees and tipping

Not for parties of seven or fewer. Parties of eight or more carry a 20 percent gratuity, which is stated when you book, printed on the menu you are handed, and shown as its own line on the check.

We add no kitchen fee, no wellness fee, no credit card surcharge and no inflation surcharge. When our costs go up, the menu price goes up where you can see it.

Appreciated, never required, and it is pooled across the floor and the kitchen and distributed by hours worked. Management takes none of it. Every employee is paid at or above their local minimum before tips.

Yes, up to six ways, and there is no charge for it. Beyond six the servers start making mistakes, so for larger groups we will happily split by item onto two or three checks instead.

Always. We are not going cashless and we do not intend to. We take cash, all major cards, contactless, Chrome Club points and our own gift cards.

Food, allergens and diets

Yes, and they are not afterthoughts. Eleven dishes are vegan as served, fourteen are gluten free as served, and several more convert with a simple substitution. Everything is tagged on the menu and you can filter the whole thing by tag.

The Neon Vegan Smash and the Sunrise Tofu Scramble are cooked on a dedicated section of the flat top with clean tools.

Tell your server before you order and tell us again in the notes if you book online. Every server can name the allergens in every dish and will take the question to the kitchen.

What we cannot do is promise a dish is free of an allergen. Our kitchens are small shared spaces where flour, nuts, dairy, egg, soy, wheat, fish and shellfish are all handled. The honest limits are set out on the allergen and nutrition page, and if the risk is severe you should read it before deciding.

All day, every day, in every room, until the kitchen closes. Pancakes at 11pm on a Friday is a normal order here and nobody will raise an eyebrow.

Guests under ten get half price portions of most of the menu rather than a separate list of six beige items. High chairs, boosters, crayons and a changing table in every restroom.

Six pies are baked in Albuquerque every morning and driven to the other rooms on Tuesdays and Fridays. That is the honest capacity of one oven and one overnight baker. When they are gone, they are gone, and we would rather that than bake something worse.

Takeout and delivery

A flat $4.95 inside four miles when you order directly from us, with no service fee, no small order fee and no markup on menu prices. Third party apps charge their own fees and mark our prices up by around 20 percent, which is their pricing and not a second menu we wrote.

Fifteen to twenty minutes for most orders. Fried chicken takes 22 minutes because it is fried from raw, and the doughnuts take eight for the same reason. We text when the bag is on the counter.

Everything except the fries. Fries are best inside eight minutes, so we pack them in a vented box on top of the bag and we will tell you honestly if the distance is too far for them. Shakes travel sealed and hold for about twenty minutes.

Gift cards and Chrome Club

No. No expiry date, no activation fee, no dormancy fee. That is our own policy and it is stricter than any state law requires. Full detail on the gift cards page.

One point per dollar, 100 points for $10 off. That is a 10 percent return, there are no tiers, points do not expire while your account is open, and there is no app to download. Free to join and free to leave.

We store your name, email, phone if you gave one, birthday month if you gave one, and your transaction history. We use it to run the program and send the emails you asked for. We do not sell it or share it for cross context behavioural advertising. Close your account and the record is deleted within 30 days. See the Privacy Policy.

Getting here and getting in

Albuquerque has a free 34 space lot behind the building with three spaces reserved for takeout and two EV chargers. Chicago is metered street parking, free after 9pm. Santa Monica guests use Structure 4 on 2nd Street where the first 90 minutes are free. Full detail on locations.

All three have step free entry, accessible restrooms and at least four tables that take a wheelchair without moving furniture. Albuquerque has a one inch lip at the patio door, which we would rather tell you about than have you discover. Tell us in the booking notes and we will hold a table that works. More on the accessibility statement.

On the patios in Chicago and Santa Monica, yes, and there is a water bowl by the door. Inside, only service animals, which is a health code requirement rather than our preference.

Free wifi in all three rooms, password on the menu. Working is fine at the counter or a two top any time except Friday and Saturday evening, when we need the tables. Nobody will move you along on a quiet Tuesday.

Events and catering

Packages are $18, $28 or $42 per guest. The Chrome Room adds a $250 room fee and a food and drink minimum of $900 weeknights or $1,500 weekends. A full buyout has no room fee but a $6,500 to $11,000 minimum. Every quote is itemised. Full detail on private events.

More than 14 days ahead and the 25 percent deposit is refunded in full. Inside 14 days we keep the deposit, because at that point we have already turned away other bookings and ordered food. Final headcount is due 72 hours before.

Yes, within 40 miles of any of our three kitchens. The griddle cart is a $600 fee on top of the per guest package, and we cook on site in front of your guests. We need a level surface and a 20 amp outlet, or we bring a generator for $120.
