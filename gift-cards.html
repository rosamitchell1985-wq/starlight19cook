      Chrome Club

      Our rewards program is deliberately boring. One point per dollar spent. One hundred points is $10 off. There is no tier system, no status to lose, no points expiry and no app to download.

      • Earning: 1 point per $1 spent on food, drink, retail and gift card redemptions. Gift card purchases do not earn points, because that would be earning twice.
      • Redeeming: 100 points for $10 off any check. Redeem as often as you like, including on the same visit you earned them.
      • Birthday: a free sundae or a free slice of pie in your birthday month. One per member per year.
      • Points never expire while your account is open. Close the account and we delete the record within 30 days.
      • Your data: we store your name, email, phone if you gave it, and your transaction history. We do not sell it. See the Privacy Policy.
