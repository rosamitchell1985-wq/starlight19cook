Getting here and getting in

  • Free lot behind the building with 34 spaces, three reserved for takeout pickup

What is only here

  • The Midnight Griddle chef's table, Thursday to Saturday at 9:30pm
  • The Chrome Room private space, seats 24
  • The pie oven. Every pie in the company is baked in this kitchen
  • Beer and wine licence only, no spirits

Holiday hours

We publish these in November each year and post them on the door of every room. Where a day is not listed, normal hours apply.

Holiday schedule, all three rooms
DayHours
Thanksgiving7:00am to 2:00pm, breakfast menu only
Christmas Eve7:00am to 4:00pm
Christmas DayClosed, the only day of the year
New Year's EveNormal hours, kitchen open till 1:00am everywhere
New Year's Day9:00am to 9:00pm
July 4Normal hours
Closed one day a year December 25 only

Merle's rule, kept since 1954: the diner opens every day the staff can safely get there. The one exception is Christmas Day, so everyone gets it at home.

If severe weather closes a room we post it on the front door, on our social accounts and at the top of this page by 6am. Chicago has closed for snow four times since 2011.
