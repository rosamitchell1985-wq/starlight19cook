How our prices work

The number on this menu is the number you pay before sales tax. We do not add a service charge, a kitchen appreciation fee, a credit card fee or a large party gratuity under eight guests. Parties of eight or more carry a 20% gratuity that is written on the menu you are handed and stated again when you book.

Third party delivery apps set their own prices and fees, which are higher than ours. Ordering from our own page always costs less.
