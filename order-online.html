      If an app suits you better

      We are on DoorDash, Uber Eats and Grubhub in all three cities. They deliver further than our two drivers can, they run later on weeknights, and they handle their own support if something goes wrong.

      They also charge us a commission of between 15 and 30 percent, which is why the menu prices on those platforms are higher than ours. That is the platform's pricing, not a second menu we wrote.

      • DoorDash, all three rooms, 7am to 11pm
      • Uber Eats, all three rooms, 7am to midnight
      • Grubhub, Albuquerque and Chicago, 8am to 10pm

      Search "Starlite Chrome Diner" in any of those apps. We do not run affiliate links to them and we earn nothing extra if you use one.

      How pickup works

      Send the order

      Use the form above or call the kitchen directly. Weekday lunch between 12 and 1 is the one time the phone may ring out, so the form is faster then.

      We text you

      You get one message confirming the order and a second when the bag is on the pickup counter. Most orders take 15 to 20 minutes. Fried chicken takes 22 minutes because it is fried from raw.

      Grab and pay

      The pickup counter is immediately left of the front door in every room. Pay by card, cash or Chrome Club points. There are three short term parking spaces reserved for pickup at each location.

      Bag on the counter 15 to 20 minutes

      Everything except the fries travels well. The fries are best eaten within eight minutes, which is why we pack them in a vented box and put them on top.
