Who we are

Starlite Chrome Diner LLC ("Starlite", "we", "us") is a New Mexico limited liability company operating three restaurants in the United States and this website at www.starlitechrome.com. We are the controller of the personal information described in this policy.

Postal address: Starlite Chrome Diner LLC, 2001 Central Avenue NW, Albuquerque, NM 87104, United States.
Email: privacy@starlitechrome.com. Phone: +1 (505) 555-0142.

What personal information we collect

Information you give us directly

  • Reservations: name, email, phone, party size, date and time, seating preference, occasion, and anything you write in the notes field, which may include dietary or accessibility information you choose to share.
  • Online orders: name, phone, chosen location, order contents, delivery address if applicable, and kitchen notes.
  • Chrome Club: name, email, optional phone, optional birth month, and your transaction history at our restaurants.
  • Gift cards: purchaser name and email, recipient name and email, amount, and the message you write on the card.
  • Contact and event forms: name, email, optional phone, and the content of your message.
  • Job applications: name, email, phone, role and location applied for, availability, and the experience you describe.
  • Newsletter: email address and consent record.

We do not collect payment card numbers on this website. Payments are handled by our payment processor and card data never reaches our servers.

Information collected automatically

  • Server logs: IP address, browser type and version, operating system, referring page, pages viewed, and timestamps. Logs are used for security and troubleshooting.
  • Essential storage: your cookie consent choice and any order in progress, stored in your browser's local storage.
  • Analytics and advertising: only if you consent. See cookies and tracking below.

Special categories

We do not ask for health information. If you volunteer an allergy, a medical dietary restriction or an accessibility need in a booking note so that we can serve you safely, we use it only for that visit and delete it 90 days afterwards.

How we use your information

  • To take, confirm and fulfil reservations, orders, events and gift card purchases.
  • To operate the Chrome Club rewards program and calculate your points.
  • To reply to your questions, complaints and job applications.
  • To send you emails you have asked for, such as the Wednesday specials list. Every one has a one click unsubscribe.
  • To keep the site and our restaurants secure, and to prevent fraud and abuse of our forms.
  • To understand which pages of this site are useful, if you have consented to analytics.
  • To show and measure advertising, if you have consented to advertising cookies.
  • To comply with tax, employment, food safety and other legal obligations.

We do not use automated decision making or profiling that produces legal or similarly significant effects.

Cookies and tracking technologies

A cookie is a small text file placed on your device. We also use local storage, which works similarly. Full detail, including a table of every cookie we set, is in the Cookie Policy.

We use three categories:

  • Strictly necessary. Remembering your cookie choice, keeping an order in progress, and protecting forms from automated abuse. These are always active because the site cannot function without them, and they do not track you across other websites.
  • Analytics. Counting visits and understanding which pages are used. Off until you turn them on.
  • Advertising. Serving and measuring ads, including through Google. Off until you turn them on.

No non-essential cookie or tracking script is set before you make a choice on our consent banner. Rejecting non-essential cookies does not reduce any functionality of this site other than advertising relevance.

You can change your choice at any time using the cookie settings link, which is also in the footer of every page. You can additionally block or delete cookies in your browser settings, and most browsers offer a "do not track" signal, which we honour as a rejection of analytics and advertising cookies where the browser sends it.

Google, advertising and how to opt out

This website displays advertising in the clearly labelled advertising slots you will see on non-legal pages. We work with Google, and Google may act as a third party vendor on this site.

  • Google uses cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this site and other sites on the internet.
  • You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.
  • You may opt out of some third party vendors' use of cookies for personalised advertising at aboutads.info and the NAI opt out page.
  • Third party vendors and ad networks other than Google may also serve ads on this site. Where they do, they are subject to the same consent gate: nothing loads until you accept advertising cookies.

If you reject advertising cookies on our banner, no personalised advertising cookies are set by us or by our advertising partners on this site.

Who we share your information with

We share personal information only with the vendor categories listed above, and:

  • With a third party delivery platform, if you choose to order through DoorDash, Uber Eats or Grubhub. In that case they are the controller of your order data and their privacy policy applies, not ours.
  • Where we are legally required to, such as a valid subpoena, court order or tax authority request.
  • To establish, exercise or defend legal claims, or to protect the safety of our staff and guests.
  • To a buyer or successor in the event that the business is sold, in which case we would notify you before your information became subject to a different privacy policy.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for cross context behavioural advertising as those terms are defined in California law.

How we protect information

The site is served over HTTPS. Access to booking, order and employment records is limited to staff who need it for their role and is logged. Payment data is handled entirely by our payment processor under PCI DSS. Backups are encrypted at rest.

No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your personal information we will notify you and the relevant authorities within the timeframes required by applicable law.

Your choices, wherever you live

Regardless of where you live, you can:

  • Ask us what personal information we hold about you and get a copy.
  • Ask us to correct anything that is wrong.
  • Ask us to delete your information, subject to records we must keep by law.
  • Unsubscribe from marketing email using the link in any email, or by writing to us.
  • Change or withdraw your cookie consent at any time.

Write to privacy@starlitechrome.com. We respond within 30 days and we do not charge a fee. We will ask you to verify your identity, usually by confirming details of a booking or order, before we act on a request.

California residents: CCPA and CPRA

If you are a California resident, the California Consumer Privacy Act as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act gives you the following rights.

  • Right to know. The categories and specific pieces of personal information we have collected, the sources, the business purpose, and the categories of third parties we disclose to. All of that is set out on this page.
  • Right to delete. Request deletion of personal information we collected from you, subject to statutory exceptions.
  • Right to correct. Request correction of inaccurate personal information.
  • Right to opt out of sale or sharing. See the section below.
  • Right to limit use of sensitive personal information. We do not collect sensitive personal information as defined by the CPRA, other than any health related dietary note you volunteer, which we use only to serve you safely.
  • Right to non-discrimination. We will never give you worse service, worse prices or fewer rewards because you exercised a privacy right.

Categories of personal information collected in the last 12 months: identifiers (name, email, phone, IP address); customer records (booking and order history); commercial information (purchases, gift cards, Chrome Club points); internet activity (pages viewed, if you consented to analytics); geolocation at city level only, inferred from IP; professional information, for job applicants only.

Categories sold or shared in the last 12 months: none.

You may use an authorised agent to submit a request. We will ask the agent for written proof of authorisation and will ask you to verify your own identity directly.

Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information

Our position, stated plainly

Starlite Chrome Diner does not sell personal information, and does not share it for cross context behavioural advertising. There is nothing for you to opt out of on that front, and that is not a technicality we intend to change quietly.

If you want to be certain, you can still tell us to treat you as opted out, and we will record that preference permanently:

  • Reject advertising cookies on the consent banner, or through the cookie settings link.
  • Email privacy@starlitechrome.com with the subject line "Do Not Sell or Share".
  • Call +1 (505) 555-0142 and ask for the privacy contact.
  • Write to Privacy, Starlite Chrome Diner LLC, 2001 Central Avenue NW, Albuquerque, NM 87104.

We honour Global Privacy Control signals sent by your browser as a valid opt out request.

Visitors from the EU, the EEA, Switzerland and the UK

We are a United States business and our restaurants are all in the United States. If you visit this site from the EU, the EEA, Switzerland or the UK, the following applies.

  • Legal bases. We process booking, order and event data to perform a contract with you. We process contact form and job application data on the basis of legitimate interests in responding to you and in hiring. We process analytics, advertising and marketing email on the basis of your consent, which you can withdraw at any time.
  • Your rights. Access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, objection, and withdrawal of consent. Write to privacy@starlitechrome.com.
  • International transfers. Your information is stored and processed in the United States. Where we transfer personal data out of the EEA or the UK we rely on the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses, and the UK International Data Transfer Addendum where applicable.
  • Complaints. You have the right to lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority, or with the Information Commissioner's Office in the UK. We would appreciate the chance to resolve it first.

Children's privacy

This website is intended for a general audience and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under the age of 13. Chrome Club membership, newsletter signup and job applications all require you to confirm you are 13 or older.

If you believe a child under 13 has given us personal information, contact privacy@starlitechrome.com and we will delete it promptly, and will confirm to you when it has been done. We comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act.

Children are of course very welcome in our restaurants. A parent or guardian makes the booking, and any information about a child that appears in a booking note is treated as the parent's information and deleted on the same 90 day schedule.

Changes to this policy

If we make a material change we will update the date at the top of this page, and where the change affects how we use information you have already given us we will email registered Chrome Club members before it takes effect. Previous versions are available on request.
