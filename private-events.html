What the price includes and what it does not

The per guest price covers food, non alcoholic drinks, service staff, equipment, setup and cleanup. Sales tax is added at the local rate. A 20% gratuity is added to every event and goes in full to the team working it, which we state here, on the quote and on the final invoice.

Alcohol is billed separately on consumption, at the same prices as the printed bar menu. There is no corkage on beer and wine you supply for a full buyout. There is no room rental hidden inside the per guest figure.

How booking an event works

Tell us the shape of it

Date, headcount, the room you want, and roughly what the evening is for. Use the form below or call the room directly. We answer event enquiries within one business day.

We send an itemised quote

Every line broken out: food per guest, staff, room fee, gratuity, estimated tax. Nothing bundled into a vague "event fee". You will know the ceiling before you commit.

Deposit and final numbers

A 25% deposit holds the date and is credited to the final bill. Final headcount is due 72 hours ahead. Cancel more than 14 days out and the deposit is refunded in full.
