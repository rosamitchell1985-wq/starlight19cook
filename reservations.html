Three ways to hold a table

Nothing here carries a booking fee, a platform charge or a per-head service charge. The deposit is a hold, not a payment.

Counter Seat

Freewalk in only

Twenty two stools along the chrome counter, first come and first served. In seventy years we have never taken a booking for one and we are not about to start.

  • No wait longer than 25 minutes, in our experience
  • Full menu, including the blue plates
  • Best seat in the house if you like watching the griddle

Booth Reservation

$10per party, credited to your check

Hold one of fourteen red vinyl booths for up to eight guests. The $10 comes off your bill when you arrive, so a booking effectively costs nothing.

  • Booths seat two to eight guests
  • Held for 15 minutes past your time, then released
  • Cancel free up to 24 hours ahead and the $10 is refunded
  • No show without notice forfeits the $10 and nothing more

The Chrome Room

$250room fee, plus a food and drink minimum

A curtained private room behind the main dining floor. Seats 24 seated or 35 standing, with its own sound system and a door onto the patio in Albuquerque.

  • Food and drink minimum: $900 weeknights, $1,500 Friday and Saturday
  • Room fee is not credited and covers setup and a dedicated server
  • Three hour block, extra hours $120 each
  • Available in Albuquerque and Chicago only

    The Midnight Griddle

    Eight stools at the pass, seven courses, cooked in front of you by Chef Delfina Arreola after the last blue plate has gone out. It is hot, it is loud, and the food is the stuff that has not made the menu yet.

    Eight stools, seven courses Thu · Fri · Sat 9:30pm
